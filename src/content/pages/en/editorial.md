Health content shapes decisions made by real people. That is why our process is explicit and verifiable.

## Who writes

Every article is written by dietitians holding an active professional registration. Authorship is always identified, with name and credential, at the start and end of each piece.

## Technical review

Articles covering supplementation, clinical conditions and quantitative recommendations are reviewed by a second professional before publication. When a reviewer is involved, they are named in the article.

## Which sources we accept

In order of priority:

1. Systematic reviews and meta-analyses
2. Randomized controlled trials
3. Guidelines from scientific societies and public health bodies
4. Large observational studies

We do not build on: personal testimony, influencer opinion, material produced by supplement manufacturers, or animal studies presented as if they were conclusions for humans.

## How we handle uncertainty

When evidence is limited or conflicting, the text says so. We prefer "the data does not yet allow us to state" over inventing a conclusion the research does not support.

## Updates and corrections

Articles are revised when relevant evidence emerges. The update date appears at the top of the text. Errors reported by readers are corrected and the change is recorded.

## Independence and advertising

This site is funded by advertising. That does not buy a single line of what we publish.

- **Ads are served by Google AdSense**, which decides what to show. We do not negotiate placements with supplement, diet or health-product brands, and we have no individual control over which advertiser appears.
- **Ads never disguise themselves as articles.** Every block is labeled "Advertisement" and is visually separated from the text.
- **No advertiser influences topics, content or conclusions.** If an article runs against an advertiser's interest, it gets published anyway.
- **An ad next to an article is not an endorsement.** Nothing appearing in an ad block has passed our editorial or technical review.
- **If sponsored content ever appears**, it will be labeled at the top of the text, with the sponsor named, and held to the same sourcing standard as everything else.
