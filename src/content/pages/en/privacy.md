This policy explains how personal data is handled at Vitualha, in accordance with Brazilian Law 13.709/2018 (LGPD).

## Three-sentence summary

We do not sell your data and we do not build reader accounts. The site is funded by Google AdSense advertising, which uses cookies to serve ads — and you can turn personalization off at any time. Beyond that, we collect only the minimum the site needs to work.

## Data we collect directly

**Browsing.** The hosting server records technical access data (IP address, date, time, page requested, browser). These logs exist for security and diagnostics and are kept for a limited period.

**Newsletter.** If you subscribe, the email address you provide is used for the sole purpose of sending content. You can unsubscribe at any time through the link in every message.

**Contact.** Messages sent by email contain whatever data you choose to include, used only to reply.

**Theme preference.** Your choice between light and dark mode lives in your browser's local storage. It is never sent to any server and disappears when you clear browser data.

## Advertising

This site displays **Google AdSense** ads. Every ad block is labeled "Advertisement" — you can always tell content from advertising.

**How it works.** Google and its partners use cookies and similar identifiers to serve ads and measure performance. Those cookies may take into account your visits to this and other sites in order to show more relevant ads. Google processes that data as its own controller — we have no individual access to it, we never receive it, and we cannot combine it with any other information.

**Your control.** You can:

- Turn off ad personalization at [Google Ad Settings](https://adssettings.google.com/)
- Review your consent choice through the panel shown on this site
- Block third-party cookies in your browser settings
- Use the aggregated opt-outs at [aboutads.info/choices](https://www.aboutads.info/choices/)

Blocking advertising cookies does **not** restrict access to any content here.

**Legal basis.** Processing for personalized advertising relies on your consent, collected through the panel shown on your first visit. Without consent, ads still appear, but non-personalized.

More detail in [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## What we do not do

- We do not sell, rent or share personal data with third parties for commercial purposes
- We do not build our own reader profiles or require accounts
- We do not use advertising data for any purpose of our own
- We do not publish paid content disguised as journalism (see [editorial policy](/en/editorial-policy/))

## Your rights

The LGPD grants you: confirmation that processing exists, access to your data, correction of incomplete or outdated data, anonymization or deletion of unnecessary data, portability, information about sharing, and withdrawal of consent.

To exercise any of these rights over data we process, write to **contato@vitualha.com**. We respond within the legal deadline. For data Google processes as a controller, the request must go directly to Google, through the channels listed in its privacy policy.

## Minors

This site is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from that age group.

## Security

The site is served exclusively over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) and applies security headers restricting the origin of any resource it loads.
