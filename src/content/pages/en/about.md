**Vitualha** exists for a simple reason: most nutrition content available today sits somewhere between incomplete and irresponsible. Easy headline, fast promise, no source.

Our standard is the opposite. Every article starts from the scientific literature, is written by a registered dietitian, and goes through technical review before publication. When the evidence is weak or contradictory, we say so instead of picking the more attractive side.

## What you find here

- **Foods:** what each food actually delivers, without superlatives
- **Nutrients:** how they work, how much is needed, where to find them
- **Health:** how diet and daily habits affect the body
- **Fitness:** nutrition applied to exercise and body composition
- **Recipes:** simple preparations that fit an ordinary day

## What you will not find

Fad diets, detoxes, miracle foods, demonized macronutrients, and sponsored copy disguised as journalism. None of it passes our editorial standard.

## An important caveat

Educational content does not replace consultation. Nutritional needs change with age, clinical history, medication and goals. For individual guidance, see a dietitian or physician.
